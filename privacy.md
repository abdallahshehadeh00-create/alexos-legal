# AlexOS Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 14, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

AlexOS may process:
- TikTok profile information, such as display name, avatar, and account identifier.
- Content and media you choose to upload.
- Application data needed to operate the dashboard.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used only to:
- Display your connected TikTok account.
- Organize your drafts and media.
- Upload content you explicitly approve to TikTok as drafts.
- Maintain the functionality and security of AlexOS.

## 3. TikTok Integration

AlexOS uses TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.

When you connect TikTok, you authorize access to the permissions displayed during login. AlexOS does not publish content automatically.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

AlexOS is designed as a locally hosted application. Application data is stored on the device running AlexOS.

TikTok access tokens are encrypted locally. Users are responsible for securing their devices and keeping their encryption keys private.

## 5. Sharing of Information

AlexOS does not sell personal information. Information is shared with TikTok only as necessary to provide the features you authorize.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can disconnect their TikTok account through AlexOS. Disconnecting removes locally stored authorization information and revokes access where supported.

Users may also delete locally stored application data.

## 7. Third-Party Services

TikTok services are governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy and terms.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as AlexOS develops.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the AlexOS project owner through the project's GitHub repository.
